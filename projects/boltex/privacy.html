2. Categories of Information We Collect

We collect information directly from you, automatically via your mobile device sensors, and through integrated third-party platforms.

A. Information You Provide Directly

  • Account Credentials: Email address, password hashes (secured via Supabase Auth), unique Supabase User ID (UUID).
  • Profile & Identity Data: Chosen username/handle, profile biography, avatar profile image URL, subscription/pro status (is_pro).
  • User-Generated Content (UGC): Photos and images uploaded to posts, textual feed posts, activity titles, activity descriptions, comments, threaded replies, post/run likes, follow/follower connections, club memberships, and challenge entries.
  • Equipment & Gear Data: Brand name, model, and cumulative distance logged for running shoes, bicycles, or other sports gear.
  • Support & Communications: Records of support tickets, bug reports, and emails sent to memorybooth.solutions@gmail.com.

B. Location Data (Precise & Approximate)

  • Real-Time GPS Coordinates: Latitude, longitude, altitude, movement speed, distance traveled, split times, and timestamped route polylines.
  • Collection Scenarios: Collected in the foreground while recording an active workout, and in the background if you navigate away from the app during an ongoing tracking session.
  • Privacy Zones: User-defined geofenced buffers that obscure exact starting and ending coordinates of public activity maps to protect your home or office address.

C. Physical Motion, Health & Sensor Data

  • Hardware Sensor Telemetry: Pedometer step counts, step cadence, physical activity recognition (ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION), and motion sensor data.
  • Workout Metrics: Activity type (running, walking, cycling, etc.), duration, active calorie burn estimates (calculated using MET values).
  • Apple Health & Google Health Connect Sync:
    • Read Access: Imported step counts and historical fitness data from Apple Health (iOS) or Google Health Connect (Android).
    • Write Access: Exported completed workout summaries (distance, duration, calories, activity type) to Apple Health or Google Health Connect upon your explicit authorization.

D. Monetization, Ad & Financial Data

  • Advertising Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID on Android) and IDFA (on iOS), managed via Google Mobile Ads (AdMob).
  • Reward Economy & Wallet Records: Gravity Coin earnings (step rewards), AdCoin balances (earned by watching video ads), ad impression events, video completion logs, and digital wallet balance transaction records.
  • Payment & Payout Information: Transaction logs, wallet redemption records, and payout destination details (bank/UPI details provided for coin redemptions).

E. Technical & Device Diagnostics Data

  • Device Characteristics: Device model, hardware manufacturer, operating system version, screen resolution, language settings.
  • Network & Connection: IP address, mobile network state, internet connectivity indicators (connectivity_plus).
  • System Logs & Diagnostics: App performance metrics, crash reports, error stack traces, and local SQLite cache logs.

Detailed Disclosures on Specific Processing Operations:

1. Targeted & Personalized Advertising (Google AdMob)

We integrate Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to deliver advertisements and reward users with AdCoins. AdMob may use device advertising identifiers (GAID/IDFA) and cookies/SDK tokens to serve targeted ads based on your interests, general region, and app interaction. You can opt out of personalized advertising at any time via your device operating system settings or in-app consent preferences.

2. Commercial Research & Aggregated Data Licensing

We may aggregate, anonymize, or pseudonymize location route data, workout telemetry, step statistics, and demographic trends. We use or share these non-personally identifiable, aggregated datasets with third-party partners, urban planners, fitness equipment manufacturers, research institutions, and data analytics firms for commercial research, product strategy, and industry benchmarks.

3. Artificial Intelligence (AI) & Machine Learning (ML) Training

We utilize anonymized or pseudonymized activity logs, route coordinates, cadence telemetry, and user interaction patterns to train artificial intelligence models, refine activity-recognition algorithms, prevent cheating/ghost-running, and develop automated AI fitness coaching tools. We do not sell raw, un-anonymized health or location data to third parties for AI training without your explicit consent.

4. Anti-Fraud & Security Enforcement

To protect our ad revenue-sharing economy, we analyze step velocity, GPS movement consistency, and ad watch intervals. Accounts detected using mock GPS spoofers, automated step shakers, ad bots, or emulator scripts will have their coin balances forfeited and accounts permanently banned.

4. Third-Party Service Providers & Data Sharing

We do not sell your personally identifiable information (PII) to third parties. We share data only with trusted service providers operating under strict data processing agreements:

  1. Supabase, Inc. (Backend Infrastructure & Authentication): Data Shared: User authentication tokens, email, encrypted profile data, uploaded photos, database records. Location: Global cloud infrastructure (US/EU).
  2. Google LLC (Google Mobile Ads / AdMob & Google Health Connect): Data Shared: Device Advertising IDs, ad impressions, health step telemetry (when authorized).
  3. Apple Inc. (Apple HealthKit): Data Shared: Workout summaries and step telemetry (when authorized).
  4. OpenStreetMap / Map Tile Providers: Data Shared: Anonymous map tile requests containing viewport bounding boxes.
  5. Legal & Regulatory Authorities: We may disclose information if required by court order, subpoena, law, or to protect the vital security of our users and platform.

5. User Privacy Rights & Choices (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, US State Laws)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of Access & Portability: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you, including your GPS run history and profile data.

6. Data Retention & Security

Retention Period

  • Active Accounts: We retain your account, profile, activity runs, posts, and coin records for as long as your account remains active.
  • Deleted Accounts: Upon receiving an account deletion request, all personal identifiers, email records, profiles, and un-aggregated GPS logs are permanently erased from our Supabase production database within 30 days.
  • Anonymized Analytics: Fully anonymized, aggregated statistical datasets (which cannot be linked back to any individual) may be retained indefinitely for historical reporting and AI model training.

Data Security Measures

We implement industry-standard technical and organizational security measures:

  • All data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.3 / SSL.
  • Production database storage in Supabase is protected with AES-256 encryption at rest.
  • Row Level Security (RLS) policies strictly isolate user database records so users can only access authorized data.
